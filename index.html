Company Overview

Crafting Lightweight Luxury Since Day One

Shree Nakoda Jewels is a leading manufacturer of super lightweight CZ casting jewelry, specializing in high-quality, modern designs built for South India's discerning taste. Every piece is produced in-house, on state-of-the-art machinery sourced from around the world, and passed through multiple quality-control stages before it ever leaves our floor.
