Who we are

A MANUFACTURER, not a MIDDLEMAN.

Shree Nakoda Jewels is a leading manufacturer of super-lightweight CZ casting jewellery, specialising in high-quality modern designs for the South Indian market.

Everything is produced in-house on state-of-the-art machinery sourced from around the world. Because we own each step — casting, stone setting, polishing, plating — we control the quality directly and move faster than a workshop that sub-contracts. Every piece passes multiple quality-control stages before it leaves our floor.

01 — Casting

WHERE weight IS engineered OUT.

Super-lightweight CZ casting is the core of what we do. Tops and rings typically sit below 3 grams, necklaces between 7 and 15, bracelets between 3 and 7 — light enough for daily wear without losing presence on the body.

02 — Quality control

CHECKED, then CHECKED again.

Our jewellery goes through multiple quality-control stages to guarantee precision, durability and finish — stone seating, polish, plating and weight are all verified before dispatch. In-house means we answer for every one of them.
